A meet-cute with a deadline

Ainsley Green has a book deadline and a fresh breakup. She enlists her best friend Lucas to help engineer chance encounters and get her writing again. The setups rarely go to plan, and the person she feels most at ease with has been beside her through every attempt.

Start here if you like rom-coms about friendship tipping into romance, awkward encounters and the slow recovery of confidence after a breakup.

About Alissa DeRogatis

DeRogatis first self-published Call It What You Want in 2023. Its online following led to a deal with Sourcebooks, which published her second novel in August 2026.
