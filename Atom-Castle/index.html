Why readers start here

The room was locked. The body was gone.

A suspect dies in police custody. When his corpse vanishes from a secured morgue, private investigator Arthur Crow is asked to explain the impossible.

In a metropolis buried beneath relentless rain, Crow works through diary accounts, police material, physical evidence and contradictions that refuse to fit. Every unusual detail matters, and the solution depends on observation rather than the supernatural.

About the author

Mystery built from evidence, not mythology.

Atom Castle is the author of Hidden in the Drizzle, a logic-driven locked-room mystery featuring private investigator Arthur Crow.

The novel combines impossible-crime mechanics, interlocking diaries, police files and a rain-soaked noir atmosphere while keeping its explanations grounded in reality.
