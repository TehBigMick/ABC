A soldier caught between an empire and a rebellion

Azetla commands soldiers in an army that denies him both rank and citizenship. His latest orders send him into the desert to capture a so-called devil. The mission draws him into a plot against the emperor, and the person he was sent to hunt may be the only ally who can get him out alive.

Start here if you like military fantasy that spends time on the price of loyalty, the manoeuvring before a battle and what empire looks like from its edges.

About J. L. Odom

Odom independently published By Blood, By Salt in 2024. The novel won the tenth Self-Published Fantasy Blog-Off in 2025 and opens a trilogy about the uprising in the southern desert.
