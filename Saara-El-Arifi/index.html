Why readers start here

Fantasy built around who gets to rule.

Saara El-Arifi’s Ending Fire trilogy uses a blood-based hierarchy to make power visible, physical and difficult to escape. Sylah, Anoor and Hassa stand in different positions within that system, and their attempts to survive it become inseparable from the question of whether it can be reformed at all.

This is one continuous epic. Begin with The Final Strife, continue through The Battle Drum and finish with The Ending Fire.
