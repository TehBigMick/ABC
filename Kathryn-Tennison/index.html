Haunted-house horror

Kathryn Tennison

Start with Molting

Michelle buys an isolated house to start over and discovers something is already nesting there.

View on Amazon About the book

How much of yourself would you give up to stay?

Michelle Callaway falls for a run-down A-frame in the New Hampshire woods. Restoring the place is supposed to give her a fresh start and a home she can make her own. As she works on it, she begins to suspect that something has settled in first.

Try Molting for an isolated setting, the familiar satisfactions of a renovation turned sour and body horror that makes the house feel closer with every chapter.

Sources include Uncomfortably Dark Horror and the Bram Stoker Awards.

About Kathryn Tennison

Tennison is an Arkansas-based horror writer whose short fiction appeared with presses including Timber Ghost and Bag of Bones. Uncomfortably Dark Horror published Molting in December 2025, and it was nominated for the 2025 Bram Stoker Award for a first novel.
