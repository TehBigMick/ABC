What happened on the climb?

Dylan wants to be the first person to climb a newly discovered Kentucky cliff. Months after her party sets off, three bodies are found under strange circumstances and Dylan is still missing. Kiefer gives away the expedition’s fate early, then lets the dread build as the climbers’ decisions catch up with them.

Pick this for a doomed expedition, a landscape that feels actively hostile and horror that gets gruesome. The climbing details make the expedition feel plausible before the story turns on its characters.

About Jenny Kiefer

Kiefer is a Kentucky climber who runs the horror bookshop Butcher Cabin Books in Louisville with her mother. This Wretched Valley was nominated for the 2024 Bram Stoker Award for a first novel.
