The bookshop she thought would be hers

Ella has spent much of her life at The Last Page and has been learning how to run it. When the owner dies, he leaves the New York bookshop to his grandson Henry. The business is in trouble, and Henry needs Ella’s help as much as she needs a say in what happens next.

Pick this if you like a slow-burn romance built around a shared problem. Ella has good reasons to be wary of Henry, and saving the shop gives them time to discover what each is willing to fight for.

About Katie Holt

Holt studied creative writing at New York University. Alcove Press published her debut, Not in My Book, in 2024, followed by The Last Page in May 2026.
