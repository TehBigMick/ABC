Why readers start here

What if the past was built to protect the future?

The Source begins with subtle changes in the Earth’s rotation and tilt. When data from distant continents starts to align, a NASA scientist and four other specialists uncover a resonance connecting some of humanity’s oldest structures.

Their investigation crosses borders, disciplines and centuries. As natural disasters intensify and powerful interests resist the truth, decoding the network becomes a race to keep the planet from reaching a catastrophic tipping point.

About the author

A global perspective behind a global story.

Dominic Nessi is an aviation-technology and cybersecurity leader, photographer and lifelong traveller who has visited more than 130 countries. His experience of real-world locations, emerging technologies and complex systems feeds directly into the scale of The Source.

The Source Trilogy brings those interests together at the meeting point of science, history and the unexplained.
