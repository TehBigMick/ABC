A heist with unfinished business

In New Imperium, magic is inked into skin and crime is a business. An assassin and a poisoner who know far too much about each other get tangled up in the same heist. The job gives them plenty of reasons to work together and even more reasons to keep looking over their shoulders.

Start with Girl Games if you want an enemies-to-lovers romance between women who can hold their own in a fight. Expect explicit romance, treachery and a heist that keeps shifting the terms of the partnership.

About Ruby Roe

Roe is a British indie author whose connected fantasy series have different couples and settings. The Girl Games books begin with a heist, while her Deals of Dark Desire duet, completed in 2026, moves into demonic bargains and dark academia.
