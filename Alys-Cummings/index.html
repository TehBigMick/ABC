A clue the police are ready to set aside

Nina is a young journalist who attends a local alcohol recovery group. When a stranger collapses and dies at one of its meetings, the police suspect an accident. The group find a clue in his pocket, and a trail of cryptic crossword clues in the newspaper gives them reason to keep looking.

Pick this if you like to puzzle things out alongside the characters. The clues are woven into the case, and the amateur sleuths have to work together while managing lives of their own.

About Alys Cummings

Cummings is a writer and journalist based on England’s north-east coast. Murder Most Cryptic won Bloody Scotland’s 2024 Pitch Perfect award before its UK publication in October 2026.
