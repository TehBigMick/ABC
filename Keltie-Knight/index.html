Why readers start here

When keeping everyone happy stops working for you.

The F*ck Them Theory brings a candid, comic voice to confidence, relationships and the pressure to keep being the bigger person. Knight draws on experiences of rejection and being underestimated to explore how setbacks can become a starting point for change.

Part personal storytelling and part practical reflection, the book pairs revealing stories with exercises about boundaries and self-worth. Its appeal is for readers who want frank conversation, sharp humour and the freedom to be imperfect.

About the author

A familiar voice with something personal to say.

Keltie Knight is a three-time Emmy Award-winning television personality, New York Times bestselling author and co-creator of the LadyGang podcast. Her work spans entertainment reporting, television, books and conversations about female friendship and ambition.

In The F*ck Them Theory, she brings that candid voice to self-worth, rejection and the pressure to meet everyone else’s expectations.
