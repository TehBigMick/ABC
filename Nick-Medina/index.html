Why readers start here

Horror with something real underneath it.

Nick Medina blends crime, mystery and supernatural horror with stories tied to local and Native folklore. The books do not treat legend as decoration: old warnings, communal memory and present-day injustice all shape what the characters face.

Sisters of the Lost Nation is the strongest first read. Indian Burial Ground is more reflective and multi-generational, while The Whistler turns a simple warning into intimate psychological terror.
