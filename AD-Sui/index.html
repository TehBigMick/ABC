A funeral rite on a lost spaceship

Vessel Iris joins a research team on a ship that vanished a thousand years earlier. He’s there to perform funeral rites, but the plants have survived and the researchers soon find themselves in danger. Iris and his AI companion must work out what else has been living on board.

Choose this for a locked-room mystery with space horror and time to think about grief, belief and what the living owe the dead.

About A. D. Sui

Sui is a Ukrainian-born speculative fiction writer. Their novella The Dragonfly Gambit won the 2024 Nebula Award for Best Novella. The Iron Garden Sutra, published by Erewhon Books in 2026, opens the Cosmic Wheel series.
