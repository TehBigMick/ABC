Why readers start here

Thrillers that do not waste time.

Freida McFadden’s books are direct, high-concept psychological thrillers. The appeal is not procedural detail; it is the uneasy feeling that someone is lying, the situation is closing in and the apparent victim may not be the whole story.

The Housemaid is the obvious first click. Never Lie offers a more isolated locked-house atmosphere, while The Boyfriend moves the threat into modern dating and obsession.
