A rivalry that began a generation earlier

When Catherine Pine arrives in Bath in 1817, her mother sees a chance to settle an old score with Lady Tisend. Catherine is supposed to catch the eye of Lady Rosalie’s prospective match. What she actually looks forward to is crossing paths with Rosalie.

Start here for a rivals-to-lovers romance with old family grudges and sharp exchanges in polite company. Bath society gives Catherine and Rosalie an audience whenever they would rather speak privately.

About Emma R. Alban

Alban is a screenwriter and USA Today bestselling author. Her Victorian Mischief & Matchmaking books began in 2024. Like in Love with You takes her queer historical romances back to the Regency.
