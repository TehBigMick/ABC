Why readers start here

The front desk sees everything.

After twenty-five years helping guests, solving problems and facing situations no employee handbook could anticipate, Deven Matthews has collected the stories that never make it into the hotel brochure.

These are 100% true encounters from hotel operations: funny, strange and unforgettable glimpses of people at their most demanding, surprising and human.

About the author

Hospitality professional, professor and storyteller.

Deven Matthews spent much of his career in hospitality, helping guests and solving problems before taking those lived lessons into the classroom.

He teaches hospitality management at the College of Southern Nevada. His first book, Hotel Operations: Practiced Theory for Future Managers, combines practical teaching with stories from the front desk; this collection makes room for the unforgettable stories that did not belong in a textbook.
