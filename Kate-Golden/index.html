Why readers start here

A developing author worth finding now.

Golden’s Sacred Stones trilogy is a clean choice for readers who want a completed romantasy arc. It starts with captivity and enemies-to-lovers tension, then broadens into a larger conflict without abandoning the emotional through-line.
