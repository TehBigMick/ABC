Why readers start here

When the life you planned no longer fits.

Renee had built a life around stability and a clear idea of success. When that future stopped feeling right, she left her career in tech and set out on two years of solo travel across fifty countries.

Unfamiliar places and unexpected challenges became a way to reconsider control, identity and trust. Returning home brought another question: how do you carry those lessons into everyday life?

This is a memoir for the space between knowing something needs to change and knowing what comes next.

About the author

From a carefully planned career to an open road.

Renee Maxine Haas is a writer, speaker and host of The Effing Audacity podcast. After leaving a career as a web developer, her solo travels changed how she understood success, uncertainty and living authentically.

Her memoir and wider work explore the courage to step beyond familiar roles, embrace change and build a life that feels like your own.
