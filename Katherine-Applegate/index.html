Why readers start here

Gentle books with real weight.

Katherine Applegate writes hopeful stories that take loneliness, displacement, cruelty and recovery seriously. Animals often provide the viewpoint, but the emotional questions are recognisably human.

Wombat Waiting is the newest entry point and a stand-alone verse novel. The One and Only Ivan is the modern classic and the beginning of a wider series. Wishtree is a warm stand-alone about community and welcome.
