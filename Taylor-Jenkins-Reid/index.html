Why readers start here

Glamour is only the surface.

Taylor Jenkins Reid uses famous or extraordinary women to explore ambition, identity, love and the stories people tell about a life after the difficult choices have already been made.

The Seven Husbands of Evelyn Hugo is the strongest general introduction. Daisy Jones & The Six uses an oral-history format and a 1970s music setting, while Atmosphere brings the emotional drama into NASA’s 1980s shuttle programme.
