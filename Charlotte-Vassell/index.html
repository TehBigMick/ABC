Why readers start here

Whodunnits with very sharp elbows.

Charlotte Vassell’s Caius Beauchamp mysteries move through wealthy London circles where reputation is currency and bad behaviour is usually protected until a body makes that impossible. The cases work as satisfying mysteries, but their bite comes from who expects to escape consequences.

Begin with The Other Half to meet Caius and the world around him. The In Crowd, winner of the 2025 Edgar Award for Best Novel, broadens the investigation; A Deadly Inheritance brings family money uncomfortably close.
