Why readers start here

Leadership advice that puts influence before authority.

Lead Last challenges familiar assumptions about taking charge. Ogunyemi instead focuses on trust, strategic vulnerability, deliberate communication, useful autonomy and the daily habits that turn goals into action.

The result is a concise guide that pairs stories and case studies with frameworks readers can apply to their own teams.

About the author

Experience from service, writing and mentorship.

Olaolu Ogunyemi is a U.S. Marine officer, mentor and award-winning writer. His leadership work draws on years of leading high-performing teams, while his wider writing reflects a long-standing interest in family connection, personal growth and developing others.
