You Don’t Own Them

A Practical Parenting Guide for Raising Humans You Actually Like

By Jonathan Smolowe

A father of three looks back on how he raised his children. Now adults, they tell him what they remember differently.

View on Amazon Explore the book

What his children remember differently

Smolowe writes about the expectations he brought to fatherhood, the mistakes he can see in hindsight and what he learnt about listening. He invites his adult children to respond to his chapters, giving them room to disagree with his account and explain how his decisions felt to them.

Through those exchanges, the book asks how parents can guide children while giving them room to develop interests, make choices and grow into adults with lives of their own.

Who it’s for

Parents and carers thinking about how much to step in, and adult readers who are curious about why parents and children can remember the same years so differently.

Jonathan Smolowe

Jonathan Smolowe is a writer, longtime business executive and father of three adult children. In You Don’t Own Them, he draws on his own family life to examine how parents build trust and how that relationship changes as their children grow up.
