Letter To Service

Defense of a Threatened Nation

By DK Holmes

A retired U.S. Air Force veteran reflects on the meaning of service and what Americans owe one another.

Get the EPUB on Payhip About the book

What does it mean to serve?

Holmes puts his experience of military service alongside reflections on the U.S. Constitution and American democracy. He considers what it means to take an oath, what service asks of people and how those responsibilities carry over into civilian life.

The memoir invites readers to think about the kinds of sacrifice they recognise, the duties they share and the legacy their own choices will leave.

About DK Holmes

DK Holmes is an American author and retired U.S. Air Force veteran. He hopes the book will start conversations among veterans, military families, educators, community leaders and other readers interested in civic life.
