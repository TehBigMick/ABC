Why readers start here

Hard problems, human answers.

Andy Weir builds suspense from engineering, limited resources and the pressure to solve the next impossible problem. His books suit readers who want the scale of science fiction without dense world-building or a slow opening.

Project Hail Mary is the strongest all-round introduction; The Martian is the purest survival story; Artemis shifts the action to a lunar city and a sharper crime plot.
