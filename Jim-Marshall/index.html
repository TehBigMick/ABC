Why readers start here

A system for bringing order to complicated behaviour.

Septemics presents a philosophical framework built around 35 scales. Each scale arranges a particular human phenomenon into seven levels, giving readers a structured way to consider how individuals and groups behave, develop and respond.

Rather than offering a conventional self-help programme, the book asks readers to learn an interconnected system, locate patterns within it and apply those observations to real situations.

About the author

The creator of the Septemics framework.

Jim Marshall is the originator of Septemics. His work draws together long-standing interests in psychology, philosophy, human development, leadership and the behaviour of groups.

The resulting book is deliberately broad: a reference-style system intended to be revisited as readers test its scales against different circumstances.
