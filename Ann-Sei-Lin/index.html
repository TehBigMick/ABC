Why readers start here

A fantasy world built to move.

Ann Sei Lin’s Rebel Skies trilogy combines East Asian-inspired world-building with airborne adventure and paper creatures called shikigami. Kurara can bring paper to life, but learning how the empire uses that power forces her to question the role chosen for her.

This is a continuing trilogy rather than three alternative entry points. Start with Rebel Skies, then follow the rebellion through Rebel Fire and Rebel Dawn.
