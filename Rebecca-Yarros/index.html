Why readers start here

Romance with teeth—and wings.

Rebecca Yarros’s Empyrean series combines the momentum of a deadly academy story with dragons, rebellion and an emotionally intense central relationship. The fantasy is easy to enter, but the consequences escalate quickly.

Unlike the other guides on this expansion, the choice is not about which book suits your mood. Fourth Wing, Iron Flame and Onyx Storm form a continuing story and should be read in order.
