Why readers start here

When loving him means losing yourself.

Actress Claire Davis has spent years performing, both on screen and in her private life. When she meets Reed Hart, the magnetic frontman of a once-popular band, she believes she has finally found something real.

But Reed is living with addiction, and Claire becomes caught between the man he is and the man he promises to become. She waits, hopes and tries to save him—until the moment she expected to change their relationship becomes her own wake-up call.

About the author

Messy lives, complicated relationships and sharp emotional truths.

Hunter Snow writes witty, heartfelt fiction about people trying to find their way back to themselves. After barely surviving a career in the music industry, she now draws on that world with a knowing eye for fame, performance and the gap between public image and private reality.

Her work centres flawed characters, difficult choices and the courage it takes to stop performing a life that no longer fits.
