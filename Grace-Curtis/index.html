Why readers start here

Big ideas, told at human scale.

Grace Curtis writes speculative fiction with an unusually gentle centre. Her novels can contain ruined planets, interstellar empires, spies, gods and sword fights, but the real momentum comes from lonely people finding connection and deciding what they owe one another.

Floating Hotel is the clearest introduction to that balance. Frontier is leaner and harsher, while Idolfire trades deep space for a mythic road journey.
