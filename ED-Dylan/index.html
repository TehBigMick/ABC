Why readers start here

Some journeys change where you end up. Others change the story of your life.

Burnt out and desperate to escape London, Edie Dylan heads to Southeast Asia for a two-month solo trip. Between beaches, unexpected encounters and moments of absurdity, there’s something that refuses to let her relax.

Thousands of miles away, Noor – a Syrian woman Edie has befriended through her refugee volunteer work – is preparing to board a smuggler’s dinghy across the Aegean Sea with her two young children. As Noor’s journey unfolds on Edie’s phone, scenes from Edie’s own past begin elbowing their way in.

About the author

Writing shaped by connection across borders.

E.D. Dylan is a British-Zimbabwean writer who spent nearly a decade volunteering online to support people displaced by war. Safe Distance is her debut memoir.

She lives with her rescue dogs and also writes poetry—an ear for voice and compressed language that suits the memoir’s blend of candour, observation and emotional depth.
