Everyone in the village knows something

It’s 1988 and Emma Daly is back in the West Cork village she thought she had left for good. When she witnesses a murder and the police arrest the wrong man, she enlists a widow, an anxious pensioner and an old flame to help untangle the village’s grudges.

Pick this for a whodunit where family history and old loyalties matter as much as the physical clues. Emma’s improvised team gives the investigation warmth, even when the village turns on itself.

About R. P. O’Donnell

O’Donnell grew up near Boston and lives in West Cork. His debut was published in the US in 2025 and earned a 2026 Mary Higgins Clark Award nomination at the Edgars. The Irish edition followed in April 2026.
